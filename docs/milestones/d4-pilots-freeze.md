# D4 — Pilots, rehearsal and freeze

Status: **PLANNED**. Planning target: 18 December 2026. Owner: maintainer + invited operators/reviewers + presenter.

## Objective and dependencies

Validate usefulness and repeatability, then freeze the presentation combination. Depends on D2, D3E/D3R and AGT1 evaluation. Roadmap: D4, AGT-02. Requirements: FR-DOC-001, FR-AGENT-001, NFR-TEST-002, COMPAT-005/006, SEC-REPORT-001.

## Deliverables

- Invite 3–5 proposed pilots; participation/access are not assumed. Record actual sample size and feedback, not invented community adoption.
- English operator/setup/troubleshooting guides, scoped compatibility matrix and limitations.
- Rehearsed live flow with sanitized offline evidence/recording fallback; chosen client/model/profile, images and source pinned.
- Prioritized defects and observed usefulness: first useful finding, confirmed findings, noise/omissions, time/reuse feedback; calibrate measures from pilots.

## Exit criteria

- [ ] Pilot findings and corrections have evidence and explicit owner; no critical defect in the demonstration flow.
- [ ] Two consecutive rehearsals pass from documented state; operator cleanup and recovery work.
- [ ] External technical review checks claims and limitations; fewer pilots require a visible scope decision, not fabricated completion.
- [ ] Model/profile and source/build freeze are recorded; later changes require relevant regression/evaluation.
- [ ] Sanitized fallback is reviewed and dated; no secrets/customer identifiers in recordings or shared documents.

No automatic outreach, publication, release/tag or environment deployment is authorized by this specification. Next: D5.
